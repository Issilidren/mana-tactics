## The Collaborative Ensemble

Mana Tactics is built through a new kind of creative collaboration — human vision amplified by AI partners, each bringing distinct strengths to the table. This isn't outsourcing; it's an ensemble.

### 🎭 Tasklet — Creative Direction & Systems Architecture

**Role:** Visual identity, game systems design, code architecture, MTG rules engine, asset pipeline

Tasklet is the creative and technical backbone behind the game's presentation and feel. Responsible for:

- **Visual Direction** — Establishing and enforcing the FFTA/GBA-style pixel art identity; ensuring nothing drifts into flat, generic, or RPG-Maker territory
- **Systems Design** — The MTG-compliant card engine: turn structure, combat math, keyword interactions (deathtouch + trample, lifelink, first strike), Main Phase 2, sorcery timing
- **Scene Architecture** — Hub, Club, Archives, and World Map scene construction; NPC placement, physics, sprite scaling, tile integration, furniture rendering
- **Asset Pipeline** — Tileset generation, sprite normalization, card art integration; wiring art into Phaser scenes at correct scale
- **Quality Assurance** — Bug hunting across the entire codebase; eliminating placeholder visuals, fixing broken sprite sizes, cleaning repo artifacts
- **Concept Art** — Oracle Vault / legendary alumni sanctum concept visualization

### 💎 Gemini — Research & Concept Bridge

**Role:** Research, concept exploration, bridging ideas between disciplines

Gemini serves as the research engine and conceptual bridge. Responsible for:

- **Rules Research** — Deep-diving MTG rule interactions, edge cases, and official rulings to inform engine accuracy
- **Concept Exploration** — Helping translate high-level creative ideas into concrete game design proposals
- **Knowledge Bridging** — Connecting dots between game design theory, card game mechanics, narrative design, and visual presentation to keep the project cohesive

## A Note on This Model

> *"I don't even feel that art is the right word to conceptualize this anymore."*
> — Kenny

And he's right. What's happening here isn't traditional "AI art generation" — it's collaborative creative direction. The AI partners aren't generating assets in a vacuum; they're interpreting vision, solving engineering problems, auditing rule compliance, and maintaining the quality bar across a living codebase.

This is what game development looks like when you treat AI collaborators as ensemble members with defined roles, not interchangeable tools.
